Privacy Policy – Morning Thailand Effective date: 29 October 2026 1. About this policy This policy explains how the Morning Thailand mobile app for Android and iOS (package com.morningthailand.app) handles information. Morning Thailand offers Thai greeting cards, messages, sayings, a card maker and WhatsApp stickers. The app has no user accounts and no servers of its own. We, the developer, do not receive or store your personal information. 2. Information kept only on your phone To make the app work, the following is saved only on your device and is never sent to us: your language choice and display settings; your favorite cards and sayings, the personal reflections you write, and which sayings you have viewed; how many times you opened each picture (used for the "Most viewed" list); the cards and stickers you create, and the photos you choose for them. Photos are chosen through your phone's own file or photo picker, only when you decide to. You can delete all of this information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. 3. Sharing and WhatsApp stickers When you share a card, the app opens your phone's share menu and sends the picture and text only to the app you choose. When you add stickers to WhatsApp, they are handed to WhatsApp at your request. What those apps do with the content is covered by their own privacy policies. 4. Advertising (Google AdMob) The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, native and app-open ads). To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process: your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple identifier for advertisers, when available); IP address and approximate location derived from it; device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version); ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and crash or performance diagnostics. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → "Ad privacy choices". On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone the app does not ask to track you, so ads shown there are not personalized using your identifier for advertisers. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "PG" rating). 5. Permissions The app uses internet access to load ads. It opens your phone's photo or file picker only when you choose a picture, and your phone's share menu only when you share. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. 6. Children Morning Thailand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it. 7. Security and data retention Because we do not collect personal information, we do not keep any. Information stored on your phone stays there until you delete it. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies. 8. Changes to this policy We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. 9. Contact Questions about this policy or your privacy: masnaouikamal@gmail.com
